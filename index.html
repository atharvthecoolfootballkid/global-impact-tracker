🤝 How Can We Help?

Poverty is influenced by many connected factors. Here are areas where communities, governments and organizations can make a difference.

📚 Education

Support access to quality education, learning materials and useful skills.

💼 Employment

Job training and skills development can help people access better opportunities.

🏥 Healthcare

Better healthcare access can reduce financial hardship caused by illness.

💧 Basic Resources

Food, clean water, sanitation and electricity are essential resources.

🏗️ Infrastructure

Roads, transportation, electricity and internet can connect communities to opportunities.

🌱 Sustainable Development

Long-term development can help communities create lasting economic opportunities.

🤝 How Can We Help?

Poverty is connected to many factors. Here are areas where communities and organizations can make a difference.

📚 Education

Support access to quality education, books, digital tools and learning opportunities.

💼 Job Skills

Support training programs that help people develop useful skills and find employment.

🏥 Healthcare

Better access to healthcare can help families avoid financial hardship caused by illness.

🍎 Basic Resources

Communities can support access to food, clean water, sanitation and other essential resources.

🏗️ Infrastructure

Roads, electricity, internet access and other infrastructure can create opportunities for communities.

🌱 Sustainable Development

Supporting sustainable projects can help communities build long-term economic opportunities.
